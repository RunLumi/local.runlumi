## Product

Customer-facing product: **Lumi Local**.

Do not brand it as Lumi Website. Do not position it as an AI website builder.

Core positioning:

> **Được tìm thấy. Được tin tưởng. Được liên hệ dễ hơn.**

Lumi Local makes a local business's online presence ready-to-use without the owner having to learn websites, hosting, SSL, domains, AI, or technical setup.

- Trust Kit: 399.000đ one-time.
- Starter: 1.990.000đ year one.

## Primary job

Get a local-business owner to take a concrete next step.

Primary CTA:

> **Xem thử Lumi Local cho business của tôi**

The CTA must use a real working enquiry path. Do not create fake checkout, fake Zalo integration, or dead buttons.

## Design

Follow `DESIGN.md` exactly.

Avoid generic AI-startup visuals: no purple gradient soup, glowing orbs, fake dashboards, unnecessary glass cards, meaningless 3D objects, or hype-heavy copy.

The page should feel premium, human, tactile, trustworthy, calm, and excellent on an iPhone.
