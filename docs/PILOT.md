# Pilot ledger and delivery checklist

Use anonymous business IDs in repository records. Keep contact details and consent in approved private operational storage, not Git. Start with an empty ledger; never seed results with invented customers.

For each of the 30 qualified businesses record: cohort, business ID, category, qualification date, owner reached, preview shown, preview human minutes, sales/travel minutes, direct Starter paid date/amount, Trust paid date/amount, credit expiry, upgrade date/additional cash, refund, delivery minutes, revision rounds, infrastructure cost, support minutes, live date, accepted deliverables and next consented follow-up. Separate cash collection from verbal interest.

At cohort close report unique denominators and raw counts, elapsed observation window, cost rate for human time, shared cost allocation, total cash net of refunds, realised contribution and remaining support reserve. Report median/p90 delivery minutes with sample size. Track day-30/60/90 activity; renewal remains unknown until actually observed. Apply STRATEGY.md gates; a small cohort is not a statistically established conversion rate.

Before collecting payment: confirm scope, digital QR versus physical printing, chosen address/domain and ownership/transfer terms, one revision, delivery date, renewal, cancellation/refund terms and authorized assets in writing.

Before publication: owner approves factual content and images; test phone, Zalo and the correct Maps destination on a real phone; scan the real printed QR to the correct business review destination; test enquiry delivery with consent; verify mobile text, pricing, hours and HTTPS. An illustrative QR in this marketing page is not a deliverable QR.

After handoff: record acceptance and instructions for requesting scoped support. Reminder dates do not authorize unsolicited messages. Respect refusals and agreed follow-up preferences. If delivery fails, stop new sales, contact the affected owner through the agreed channel, restore the last working version and agree a remedy; record the failure without PII.
