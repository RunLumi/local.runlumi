Five evidence classes were checked: (1) official platform documentation, (2) Vietnamese commercial suppliers, (3) free tools/DIY alternatives, (4) editorial guides/examples, (5) forums/community evidence. They are not equally authoritative and do not all appear prominently for every query. Industry searches also showed irrelevant architectural-design results and mixed geographic targeting; those were excluded. Community posts are anecdotal/promotional, not customer demand or causal outcome proof.

## Primary authorities and source limits

Platform copy follows [Google review invitations](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), [Maps contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114), [local ranking factors](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) and [private profile performance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9918094). Metadata follows [localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions) and [canonicalization](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization). Sources accessed 2026-10-06. No external source is treated as permission to add scope, quote outcomes or use photos.

Customer pages use the repository's current offer and prices; search results do not alter them. English pages target operators in Vietnam, but their demand is unmeasured. The blog is educational; service pages describe real deliverables. There are no commercial verification, suspension recovery, recurring Local SEO or district doorway pages.

## Cheapest test and stop conditions

Within the first 30 days after authorized publication, inspect indexed pages and query/locale data if Search Console is authorized. At 200 relevant impressions and CTR below 1%, review snippet/intent; at 100 qualified landing sessions and zero enquiries, review offer/CTA fit. These are the goal's diagnostic thresholds, not predictions or industry benchmarks. If Maps enquiries mostly demand excluded work, tighten framing before expanding scope or pages. No tracker was added; only allowlisted non-PII intent reaches the existing intake.
