## Objective

Use AI to make website production cheap while selling a **done-for-you** service that a local-business owner does not need to learn, publish, or operate.

> **Do not sell code. Do not sell “AI website”.**
>
> Sell a respectable online presence with minimal owner effort.

Core loop:

**Google Maps → AI research + mini preview → field rep goes in person → Trust Kit 399k or Lumi Local Starter 1.99m → annual renewal → Lumi Ops when real pain appears**

## Strategic premise

AI-generated websites will become cheaper and easier. Lumi must not depend on “website building is hard” as the value proposition.

Customers should still pay Lumi even if they can generate a website for free because Lumi finds the business, understands context, prepares a relevant preview, handles setup, gets domain/hosting/SSL working, connects Call/Zalo/Maps/QR, publishes and maintains the site, and can later connect real lead workflow to Lumi Ops.

### Moat

> **Distribution → Trust → Ownership → Workflow**

- **Distribution:** Google Maps + field reps reach real businesses.
- **Trust:** show something relevant and use Trust Kit as a low-risk paid step where useful.
- **Ownership:** operate website/domain/monitoring, not merely hand over code.
- **Workflow:** after trust exists, learn how the business handles leads/jobs and sell Lumi Ops only when pull is real.

Website is the bridge, not the moat.

## Product brand

The customer-facing brand is **Lumi Local**.

Do not say “buy Lumi Website”.

Say:

> **Lumi Local giúp business có website, Google review, Call/Zalo và phần hiện diện online mà chủ business không phải tự setup.**

## Trust Kit

Customer gets:

- Google Review QR;
- standee artwork/file;
- short public-presence audit;
- public Maps/info checklist;
- review-request message template;
- Call/Zalo/Maps QR where appropriate;
- Free Local Presence Alerts where implemented.

Lumi does not ask for Google Business Profile edit access, log into the customer's Google account, manually manage Maps, reply to reviews as a managed service, buy/incentivize reviews, or promise ranking.

Alerts are public-data notifications, not profile management.

## Lumi Local Starter

**1.990.000đ year one**

Includes:

- mobile-first website / landing page;
- content prepared from verified business information;
- Call / Zalo;
- Google Maps;
- enquiry form;
- hosting + SSL;
- basic local-search structure;
- one basic revision round;
- RunLumi address or custom domain;
- lead form capture;
- alerts/monitoring where implemented.

Not included by default:

- unlimited custom design;
- branding/logo;
- ads;
- social management;
- custom apps;
- deep integrations;
- SEO ranking guarantee;
- lead/revenue guarantee.

AI should increase gross margin, not increase scope.

## Preview before the visit

For a qualified prospect, AI prepares a mini Lumi Local preview before the field visit.

Preview can include business name, verified public logo/image, headline, 2–3 services, Call, Zalo, Maps, and mobile layout.

Rules:

- automate first;
- average human time <=5 minutes/prospect;
- no deep customization;
- clearly illustrative, never pretend it is an official customer site.

## Sales flow

### Owner likes the preview

> **Bên em làm trọn Lumi Local Starter gồm website + QR review + Call/Zalo + Maps + form + hosting năm đầu là 1.990.000đ.**

### Owner is hesitant

> **Nếu anh/chị muốn thử nhỏ trước, bên em có Trust Kit 399 nghìn. Nếu nâng cấp Lumi Local Starter trong 30 ngày thì 399k được trừ toàn bộ.**

## Renewal

From year two:

- **599k/year:** RunLumi subdomain + hosting + SSL + maintenance.
- **999k/year:** one standard custom domain + hosting + SSL + maintenance.

For a serious operating business, custom domain is the natural recommendation. Do not pressure the customer.

## ICP

Prioritize businesses that are clearly operating, have Google Maps, have recent reviews/photos/activity, have a real address/contact route, have no site or a weak site, have an accessible owner/manager, and sell services where one customer has meaningful value.

Examples: HVAC, garage, spa/salon, dental/clinic, pet services, interior/home repair, cleaning, pest control, studio, laundry, education.

## Reviews

Only invite genuine customers to share honest experience.

Never use a discount/gift for reviews, buy reviews, fabricate reviews, ask for five stars, or review-gate.

## Expansion

After website is live ask:

> **Khi khách gọi, Zalo hoặc gửi form thì ai theo dõi? Có cách nào biết khách nào chưa được xử lý không?**

Only then consider lead inbox, assignee, next action, reminders, quotations, jobs, recurring service, owner dashboard, and automation.

Do not use Lumi Ops as the cold-sales hook.

## Initial test

Cohort: **30 qualified local businesses**.

Continue if:

- >=6/30 produce a paid signal;
- >=2 Starter paid;
- average human preview time <=5 minutes;
- delivery remains standardized;
- manual Google work is near zero.

Rewrite if Trust Kit sells but Starter does not, previews are liked but nobody pays, Starter becomes bespoke, or renewal support becomes heavy.

Do not scale field headcount because Trust Kit alone sells.

## Resilience test

Every quarter ask:

> **Nếu ngày mai khách có thể tự generate website đẹp miễn phí trong 60 giây, họ còn trả Lumi không?**

The answer must be yes because Lumi does the full setup, publishing, domain/hosting, maintenance, alerts, and relationship.

## Guardrails

- do not sell “AI website” as a moat;
- no fake discounts;
- no Google edit access;
- no manual Maps-management drift;
- no ranking/lead/revenue promise;
- no unlimited custom work;
- no domain lock-in;
- no scaling before repeated **Lumi Local Starter Paid**.
