# RUN-066 — Earned-resource channel discovery

Observed 2026-10-07. One bounded search for Vietnam small-business resource/editorial pages and public contribution rules. Read-only; no contact, account interaction, link request, or draft submission occurred.

## Results

- HCM Small and Medium Enterprises Association: [hcmsme.net](https://hcmsme.net/) search results show news, media/case-study categories, business listings and a “Liên kết website” area, but no public guest-contribution or external-link submission criteria. A direct in-app browser open on 2026-10-07 failed with `net::ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID`; the site was not opened and no TLS warning was bypassed. The exact listing rules remain unknown.
- Small and Medium Enterprise Research and Support Center (SMERS): [smers.org.vn](https://smers.org.vn/) presents support activities, policy/news content and contact information. A targeted site search for public article-submission or contributor rules found no result; that does not prove no private process exists.
- Business Support Center: [vietnamsme.gov.vn](https://www.vietnamsme.gov.vn/) presents SME services, training and digital-transformation support. The search did not surface an open editorial contribution process.

A follow-up search of the center's [Dữ liệu số page](https://vietnamsme.gov.vn/du-lieu-so/) describes data about businesses and support activities; it is not a publicly documented service-provider directory or editorial submission form. The result therefore does not create an eligible listing opportunity.

Searches included “Vietnam small business resource center contributor submit article guidelines local business owners” and site-restricted Vietnamese terms for article submission, contributors and media collaboration. Search snippets and page categories are discovery evidence only; they do not establish audience fit, permission or acceptance criteria.

## Decision

No candidate meets the campaign's gate for an earned editorial/resource link: a relevant page, a visible submission process, allowed link policy and a genuinely useful Lumi resource could not all be verified. Do not contact these organizations or treat membership/listing pages as free-link opportunities. Revisit only if an explicit public submission process or user-authorized relationship becomes available. The campaign's three Reddit comment scopes remain consumed; no new channel scope is inferred.
