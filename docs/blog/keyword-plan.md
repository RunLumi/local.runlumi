## Why this order

Publish one comparison pillar and four complementary guides, paired in English at /en/blog/. First test the comparison and honest-review intent because they can discuss Lumi deliverables directly. SEO has estimated demand but sharply negative YoY in this export; verification asks for work Lumi excludes. They earn useful education only if owner expectations remain clear. The table's experiment order is judgment, not a derived probability of ranking.

Commercial variants such as dịch vụ seo google map (590) belong within the SEO decision guide, not another near-duplicate URL. The setup cluster includes tạo địa điểm trên google map (390) and cách tạo địa điểm trên google map bằng điện thoại (480); these figures are not added together.
