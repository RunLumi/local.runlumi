# UI/UX research notes — Lumi Local

Updated: 2026-10-03

This note records the external references used for the October 2026 Lumi Local UI/UX refinement. It is not a license to copy visual assets or source code. We borrow interaction and information-design principles, then express them through Lumi Local's own Civic Editorial system.

## References reviewed

### Linear

- Homepage: https://linear.app/
- 2026 design refresh: https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh

Useful principle: not every element deserves equal visual weight. Navigation and supporting structure should recede so the user's current task stays dominant. Linear also explicitly argues that structure should be felt rather than seen, using fewer and softer separators.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- fewer equal-weight cards;
- softer borders;
- quieter navigation;
- stronger visual hierarchy around the single core offer;
- fewer decorative icons.

### Stripe

- Homepage: https://stripe.com/

Useful principle: pair a concise value proposition with tangible product artifacts, then reveal detail progressively. Stripe uses large product surfaces and strong numeric anchors without making every section equally loud.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- the 1.99m Starter offer becomes the dominant pricing object;
- supporting offers become side rails rather than three competing cards;
- product visuals do more explaining than feature lists.

### Framer

- Homepage: https://www.framer.com/
- Design product: https://www.framer.com/design/

Useful principle: show the product in context and make responsiveness visible. Framer repeatedly uses realistic visual canvases instead of abstract decoration.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- hero uses inspectable HTML product artifacts, not a generic illustration;
- local-business demo surfaces show how Lumi Local looks in context;
- mobile composition is treated as the primary product proof.

### Square

- Websites: https://squareup.com/us/en/online-store
- Website builder: https://squareup.com/us/en/website-builder

Useful principle: put the business outcome and actual product front and center; make the next action obvious; explain the relationship between website, domain, contact and growth without forcing technical language.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- show the website before the feature catalogue;
- simplify the path to one primary CTA;
- keep custom domain visible as a natural professional upgrade.

### Toast

- Websites: https://pos.toasttab.com/products/websites/

Useful principle: lead with the business's online home, then connect it to the real customer journey. Toast also asks visitors to identify business type, which creates relevance before deep explanation.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- add a local-business example rail for Spa, Garage and Điện lạnh;
- examples are explicitly illustrative, never fake customer proof;
- industry relevance appears before the pricing explanation.

### Housecall Pro

- Websites: https://www.housecallpro.com/features/websites/

Useful principle: “built for you” is stronger for busy service-business owners than “here is another builder.” The owner should feel that the provider handles setup and launch.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- done-for-you language stays central;
- technical implementation is deliberately secondary;
- setup, publishing and maintenance are framed as the thing the owner avoids doing.

### Squarespace

- Websites: https://www.squarespace.com/websites
- Start: https://www.squarespace.com/start

Useful principle: strong curated examples reduce imagination cost. The product is easier to buy when the visitor can immediately see a version that resembles their own business.

Applied to Lumi Local:

- curated demo business cards replace a generic “fits these industries” list;
- visual craft itself becomes proof of what Lumi can deliver.

## Resulting UX decisions

1. Hero must show an inspectable Lumi Local artifact, not only an illustration.
2. Core Starter price is visually dominant; Trust Kit is a risk-reduction sidecar.
3. Renewal is shown once in a compact receipt-like rail, not repeated as a competing product.
4. Add an industry relevance rail before pricing.
5. Replace six equal feature cards with a bento-like hierarchy so the website remains the primary component.
6. Reduce form friction: name, business and phone/Zalo first; email and note are optional details.
7. Keep a compact CTA in the mobile header.
8. Use fewer borders and quieter surfaces. Structure should be felt, not loudly outlined.
9. Do not add fake social proof to imitate large brands.
10. Preserve Lumi Local's own Paper / Civic Navy / Lumi Blue system and exactly two glass controls.

## Rejected patterns

- AI-first hero messaging;
- autoplay video;
- giant animated gradients;
- fake dashboards;
- testimonial carousels without real customers;
- ranking / lead / revenue guarantees;
- large feature matrices;
- equal-weight pricing tiers that make the entry path ambiguous;
- sticky mobile bars that cover content.

The target reaction remains:

> “Đẹp. Dễ hiểu. Giá này hợp lý. Họ làm hết cho mình.”
