# Lumi Local positioning

## Product

**Lumi Local** is the customer-facing product.

Website is a core component, not the identity of the product.

Do not sell:

> “Lumi makes websites with AI.”

Sell:

> **“Lumi Local làm trọn phần hiện diện online cho business của bạn.”**

The owner should not need to learn hosting, SSL, publishing, a website builder, or Google tooling.

## Core promise

> **Được tìm thấy. Được tin tưởng. Được liên hệ dễ hơn.**

Lumi prepares, the owner reviews, Lumi publishes and maintains the agreed technical layer.

## Why this survives cheap AI website generation

The cost of generating a website is falling. That is good for Lumi's gross margin but is not a moat.

The durable advantage is:

**Distribution → Trust → Ownership → Workflow**

- **Distribution:** find real local businesses via Google Maps and field outreach.
- **Trust:** show a relevant preview and offer a small Trust Kit when useful.
- **Ownership:** operate the website/domain/monitoring layer rather than merely hand over code.
- **Workflow:** after trust exists, discover real lead/follow-up pains that may justify Lumi Ops.

## Target customer

Vietnamese local businesses, HKDs, and SMEs such as:

- salon / spa;
- garage / auto repair;
- HVAC / air conditioning;
- dental / clinic;
- pet services;
- interior / home repair;
- cleaning / pest control;
- laundry;
- studio / photography;
- education centers.

Prioritize businesses that are clearly operating, have Google presence/reviews, have a real address/contact route, and have no website or a weak one.

## Product ladder

**399k Trust Kit → 1.99m Lumi Local Starter → 599k/999k annual renewal → Lumi Ops**

Trust Kit is a risk reducer, not a mandatory toll gate. If the owner likes the preview and is ready, close Starter directly.

## Resilience test

Ask quarterly:

> If a customer can generate a beautiful website for free in 60 seconds tomorrow, would they still pay Lumi?

The answer must be based on done-for-you setup, local distribution, publishing, domain/hosting, maintenance, alerts, and the relationship, not on code being hard.
