**Updated:** 2026-10-03

### Credit rule

If a Trust Kit customer upgrades to Lumi Local Starter within **30 days**, credit the full **399.000đ**.

Remaining amount:

> **1.591.000đ**

Do not use fake strikethrough pricing.

## Trust Kit scope

- Google Review QR;
- standee artwork/file;
- short public-presence audit;
- public checklist;
- review-request message template;
- contact/Maps QR assets where appropriate;
- Free Local Presence Alerts where implemented.

Trust Kit does **not** include manual Google Business Profile management.

## Lumi Local Starter scope

- mobile-first website / landing page;
- content prepared from verified business information;
- Call / Zalo;
- Maps;
- contact form;
- hosting + SSL for year one;
- basic local-search structure;
- one basic revision round;
- RunLumi subdomain or own domain depending on setup;
- monitoring / public-data alerts where implemented.

## Renewal

### 599.000đ/year

- RunLumi subdomain;
- hosting;
- SSL;
- technical maintenance within scope.

### 999.000đ/year

- one standard custom domain;
- hosting;
- SSL;
- technical maintenance within scope.

Premium, aftermarket, or unusually priced domains are quoted separately.

For an established business, custom domain is the recommended option, but 599k remains a real fallback.

## Scope guardrails

Not included by default:

- branding/logo strategy;
- ads;
- social media management;
- unlimited revisions;
- custom application development;
- deep integrations;
- SEO ranking guarantees;
- lead/revenue guarantees.

Extra scope must be quoted before work begins.

## Economics to measure

For every paid customer track:

- acquisition price;
- cash collected;
- Trust Kit hard cost;
- domain cost;
- hosting cost;
- human sales time;
- preview human time;
- website delivery time;
- support time;
- rework/refund;
- renewal plan;
- Lumi Ops opportunity.

AI should reduce delivery cost and increase gross margin. It must not silently expand scope.

## Current falsification gate

Initial cohort: **30 qualified local businesses**.

Continue only if:

- >=6/30 produce a paid signal (Trust Kit or Starter);
- >=2 Lumi Local Starter paid from the cohort;
- average human preview time <=5 minutes;
- delivery remains standardized;
- manual Google work remains near zero.

Do not scale field headcount just because Trust Kit sells.

## Scope clarification — 2026-10-04

Both packages supply print-ready QR artwork, not physical stands, printing or shipping. Starter includes this artwork and one basic content revision round before publication. Confirm the chosen first-year address/domain, eligibility, ownership, cost and transfer terms in the written scope before collecting payment. The listed renewal does not cover every possible .vn/.com registration.

No verified alert implementation is established by this marketing repository. Do not include alerts in an unconditional customer promise. Technical maintenance covers the agreed site, hosting and SSL, not ongoing content, marketing or Google profile management.

The 30-business gate is permission for another bounded learning cohort, not evidence of product-market fit. Use the contribution and delivery gates in STRATEGY.md before expanding headcount.
