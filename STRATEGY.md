# Lumi Local: earn the right to scale

Decision record, 2026-10-04. Status: acquisition hypothesis; market demand and repeatable contribution are **unproven**.

## Decision and strongest objection

Sell a mobile presence package built for the owner: show a relevant private preview, offer Starter directly, use Trust Kit only when the owner needs the smaller deliverable. Prove this with 30 qualified businesses in one nearby service category before increasing sales headcount.

The strongest objection: an owner may already get enough business through Maps, Facebook and Zalo. A better-looking website may solve no urgent problem. A polite reaction to a free preview is not willingness to pay. Cheap generation can also make unsolicited previews feel generic and expendable. We choose a bounded paid pilot because it tests this objection cheaply; the website alone cannot answer it.

## What is known, inferred and unknown

**Repository facts:** the offer is 1.99m VND for Starter, 399k for Trust Kit with full 30-day credit, and 599k/999k renewals. This repo implements marketing and enquiry intake. It is not evidence of an automated customer-site factory, live alerts, paid demand, retention or contribution margin.

**External observations:** [Mono Quick Creator](https://www.monosolutions.com/platform/quick-creator-ai) describes generating prospect websites for sales conversations. [Nhanh website pricing](https://nhanh.vn/bang-gia-website) is a local comparator, not proof that Lumi can acquire or retain its buyers. Sources accessed 2026-10-04. Vendor success claims are not independent causal evidence. Competitor price comparisons must be refreshed before external use and must compare scope, tax, support and renewal terms.

**Inference:** the preview can reduce the effort needed to understand the offer. Done-for-you execution may matter more than the generation technology. Confidence: moderate in the mechanism, low in local conversion and economics until tested.

**Unknown:** reachable buyers, current sales, delivery capacity, first-year domain cost, printing expectations, support load, refunds, response staffing, real renewal, and willingness to pay. No forecast assumes these are already solved.

The [original comparison research](docs/archive/STRATEGY-RESEARCH-2026-10.md) is preserved for reference. Its opaque search citation IDs, competitor figures, proposed commissions and 8→12-rep rollout are historical hypotheses, not active instructions or verified market facts.

## One wedge and one offer

Pilot recommendation, not a claimed market fact: independent garages in one nearby district where the founder can reach the owner. Qualify active businesses with recent customer activity, a weak/missing mobile service page, meaningful service jobs, and a reachable decision-maker. If founder access is stronger in another service category, choose that category once and record why before the cohort begins. Do not spread 30 prospects across ten industries.

The buyer must identify a concrete friction: people cannot find service scope, prices, directions, or a reliable contact route. Ask about the last actual enquiry that ran into this friction. Do not lead with “would a prettier site help?” Keep Facebook, Maps and Zalo; the website connects them.

Starter first. Trust Kit is a useful standalone QR/checklist purchase, not evidence that the customer wants Starter. Keep current prices and full credit constant. Do not split this tiny pilot into simultaneous price, preview, credit and alert tests.

## Deliverable and ownership boundary

Use COPY.md and PRICING.md. QR artwork is print-ready; physical production is not included. One basic revision is included. Alerts are not guaranteed until implemented and verified. No Google credentials, edit access, review gating, five-star requests, ranking or sales guarantees.

Preview is private and marked “draft, not the official website.” Public source data does not confer image rights. Use authorized assets and owner-confirmed facts; do not publish prospect sites, activate forms, or buy domains before approval. Before payment, agree deliverables, domain cost/ownership/transfer, delivery date, revision scope, renewal and cancellation/refund terms in writing. Do not invent a refund guarantee without an operating policy.

## Economics and gates

Use unique qualified businesses as the cohort denominator. Separately count unique owners reached, previews shown, direct Starters, Trust-only purchases and later upgrades. Count an upgrading business once as a Starter. Interest and form submissions are not paid signals. Observe the full 30-day credit window before calling upgrade failures.

Contribution = cash collected − refunds − acquisition costs − domain/hosting/printing actually incurred − generation/payment costs − delivery/support labour at an explicit hourly cost. Include founder time, unsuccessful previews and visits. Report realised contribution separately from estimated remaining year-one support. Do not count year-two renewal as year-one revenue or sum Trust Kit plus full Starter price after credit.

Initial gates are internal decisions, not industry benchmarks:

- Continue to a second bounded cohort only if at least 6/30 businesses pay and at least 2 become paid Starters, average human preview work is at most 5 minutes, and no manual Google management is needed.
- Before adding reps, reproduce Starter sales in a second cohort; target variable CAC ≤600k VND per Starter, median fulfillment ≤120 minutes and p90 ≤240 minutes, with positive estimated year-one contribution after labour and support reserve. Publish the actual observations and costing assumptions. Unknown costs fail the scale gate.
- Keep the whole cohort cost separate from Starter-only economics; disclose how shared costs are allocated and also show the conservative result charging all prospecting costs to Starters.
- Pause acquisition if promises exceed capacity, customers misunderstand physical delivery, or paid delivery is loss-making. Fix scope before recruiting. Never retroactively remove earned credit.

Two Starter sales are learning evidence, not product-market fit or statistical proof. Zero paid Starters after 20 qualified owner conversations triggers a pitch/pain/ICP review. Strong Trust-only sales with no upgrades after 30 days require evaluating Trust Kit as a separate demand, not hiring more reps.

## Next seven days

1. Days 1–2: founder selects one cluster, rehearses the scope, lists 30 qualified businesses, and creates three private previews within the time limit. Set an affordable cash/time cap before outreach; preserve agreed family and rest time.
2. Days 3–5: founder conducts the first ten owner conversations; record the last real customer-contact problem, preview response, objection, paid outcome and consented next step. Revise one weak part of the pitch, with its date recorded.
3. Days 6–7: deliver any paid work, measure labour and costs, test actual Call/Zalo/Maps/form routes, and compare promises with delivery. Finish the cohort without expanding scope. No automated outreach is authorized by this plan.

Use [the pilot ledger](docs/PILOT.md). The immediate physical action is to choose the first district and ten owner-accessible garages; repo polishing is no substitute for these conversations.

## Tradeoffs and second-order risks

A narrow vertical gives faster learning but may miss a stronger nearby segment. A private preview reduces imagination cost but can consume unpaid labour. A low price shortens some decisions but attracts bespoke support unless scope is visible. Recurring hosting can create trust or coercive lock-in; clear ownership and transfer terms are mandatory. Sales incentives should reward paid, correctly scoped delivery rather than QR volume, but commission amounts remain unapproved experiments.

Lumi Ops follows observed recurring workflow pain and a separate buying decision. It is not a reason to build a CRM now. No headcount schedule, paid Care product, mass prospect scraping, or automatic price change is approved by this strategy.
